The TD Process

How every session is structured

1

Assess

Warm-up and quick assessment of your focus for today—what do you want to work on?

2

Personalize

Technical drills tailored to your specific needs. 1v1, 2v2, game-like scenarios.

3

Practice

Real-game pressure situations. You train like you play—decisions, positioning, execution.

4

Game Situation

Match-intensity scenarios with pressure and consequence. This is where you prove it.

5

1-on-1 Contact

6

Video Review

Watch footage together. See exactly what you did well, what to improve, how it translates to matches.
